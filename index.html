About us

A place to learn, ask and grow

Many young people want to understand their faith but do not know where to begin, or feel unsure about asking. RHYC offers regular dars where every question is welcome and every lesson is connected to daily life.

Our focus is simple: sound basics, good manners, and a community that supports each other. Classes are led by a qualified teacher, with the knowledge of parents and guardians.

Our path

From learning to living it

  1. 1

    Recite

    Learn to read the Quran correctly and with confidence.

  2. 2

    Understand

    Study the meaning, and the life of the Prophet behind it.

  3. 3

    Practise

    Build prayer, honesty and good manners into each day.

  4. 4

    Share

    Serve the community and invite others with kindness.

Our promise to every family

Guardian consent

Every student under 18 joins only with a parent or guardian's permission and contact number.

Open and supervised

Classes happen in open, shared spaces, and no adult is ever alone with a child.

Private information stays private

We collect only what we need, and only the organizers can see it.
